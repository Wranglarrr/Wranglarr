# Service icons

From [dashboard-icons](https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons) (Apache License 2.0),
taken 2026-10-26. `postgres.svg` is their `postgresql.svg`.

The logos are trademarks of their owners. wranglarr shows them only to identify the service
a notification channel or container talks to; that implies no endorsement.

The app logos (`radarr.svg` … `seerr.svg`) and the media servers' (`plex.svg`, `jellyfin.svg`, `emby.svg`,
`kodi.svg`) come from the same set, taken 2026-10-26. wranglarr shows an app's own logo, asked from the app,
and falls back to these when it can't get one.

`email.svg` is Lucide's `mail` icon ([lucide.dev](https://lucide.dev), ISC license), coloured.
