Passkeys

A passkey signs you in without the password, with your fingerprint, face, device PIN or a security key. It counts as two factors on its own, so it skips the authenticator code. Passkeys stay on your devices (or in your password manager); wranglarr only keeps their public keys.

API keys

For scripts and other tools: send the key as the X-Api-Key header to any /api/ address. A key can do everything the API can, except change this account. It's shown once; wranglarr keeps only a hash.
