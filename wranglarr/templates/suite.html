What a downloads backup holds

wranglarr backs up the torrents and NZBs themselves, not what they downloaded. The downloaded files live on the shared /data mount (/data/torrents, /data/usenet), the same place the arrs import from and the same disk as your media, so they belong to your normal file backups, not here. A backup of a download client is a few kilobytes per torrent or NZB, however large the download.

After a restore the client finds its torrents and jobs again: torrents re-check the data already on /data and carry on (or download again if it's gone); an interrupted usenet job can be added again from its saved NZB.
